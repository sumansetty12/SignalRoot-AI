# SignalRoot AI

**Tagline:** Understand incidents. Find root causes. Restore reliability.

**Purpose:** Help small engineering teams investigate production incidents using synthetic or authorized telemetry, evidence-backed root-cause hypotheses, and safe recommendations.

**Repository name:** `signalroot-ai`

This is an early research prototype, not a production incident-response system. Do not include employer code, confidential architecture, customer data, secrets, or unredacted logs.
